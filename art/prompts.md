# Illustration prompts

All illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and encoded as WebP for the game.

## Main screen

Use case illustration-story. Original landscape 16:9 raster key art for a funny Taiwanese xianxia browser game. Hand-painted East Asian ink-and-gouache animation background, textured brushwork, dark indigo storm, misty jade crags and warm gold talisman light. On the right-center a goofy young adult Daoist in a jade teal robe, small black scholar hat flying off, wide alarmed eyes, scrambling awake from a grassy nap, one cloth shoe flying. Around him a glowing circular bagua formation lies on a summit above clouds, immense forked gold and pale blue lightning arcs across the sky. Wide cinematic composition with lots of attractive readable visual detail on the right half. Left third predominantly deep dark indigo and mist with low contrast so live HTML UI can overlay it. Original and comedic, strong staging and painterly grain. No lettering, symbols that look like writing, logo, watermark, frame or typography.

## Ending illustrations

Each scene below was generated separately with this shared prompt.

Use case illustration-story. Generate ONE complete landscape 3:2 raster illustration for an illustrated ending card in an original comedic Taiwanese xianxia browser game. Character is a young adult East Asian Daoist with a messy black topknot, small black scholar hat, expressively arched eyebrows, jade teal robe with gold trim, and cream inner robe. Painterly East Asian ink-and-gouache storybook animation art, layered atmospheric brush textures, expressive comedy, dark ink shadows and warm highlights. Mid-distance composition with primary visual joke close to center and ample context.

The prompt ends with “No text, letters, captions, watermark, frame, UI or grid.”

- **01-sweep**. At dusk the Daoist sheepishly sweeps fallen gingko leaves at a mountain temple gate, clutching a straw broom, hair lightly singed. One leaf is stuck to his hat.
- **02-foundation**. The Daoist proudly wedges an absurdly magnificent jade foundation brick under the wobbling leg of a battered wooden chair in a courtyard, holding it level with both hands.
- **03-golden-pill**. An alchemical pill gleams like a miniature sun between the Daoist's tongs above a ceramic furnace. A fellow cultivator in the background eyes it like a jewel, and his sleeves are soot-smudged.
- **04-bath**. A tiny glowing spirit double with the young Daoist’s face levitates above an old wooden bath tub and reaches for a towel. The Daoist, fully dressed, peeks anxiously around the screen. Warm steam and playful faces.
- **05-incense**. A bewildered Daoist in jade robes sits rigidly like a deity on a temple dais, surrounded by incense and offerings, while a devoted visitor proffers a long blank prayer slip. Comic expression, curling smoke.
- **06-void**. The Daoist stands on a stool trying to patch an impossible swirling purple void in a temple roof with a wooden shingle. Warm lamplight and absurd cosmic blue starlight.
- **07-sword**. The Daoist and his animated jade flying sword pull in opposite directions on a household rent pouch at a wooden doorway, a comic standoff; the sword moves as if it has an attitude.
- **08-recruitment**. The Daoist recoils clutching a scorched bent lightning rod while three elders in different sect robes lean in offering unmarked silk appointment scrolls. Comedic crowding, temple veranda.
- **09-leave**. The Daoist holds out a blank gold-edged leave request to a stern thunder bureaucrat seated behind a desk among storm clouds. Lightning flickers behind the inkstone, the Daoist smiles nervously.
- **10-wanderer**. The wandering Daoist lounges on a misty pine bough with a large amber drinking gourd, then looks over his shoulder at a celestial courier with a long unmarked official scroll.
- **11-earth-shrine**. The newly appointed local earth immortal stands at a tiny red mountain shrine holding a broom and dustpan. A comically oversized fragment of stray golden lightning lies in the dustpan.
- **12-cloud-carriage**. The Daoist nervously boards an ornate old wooden sky carriage floating on soft clouds above jade mountains, clutching an oversized cloth-wrapped sword as carry-on. The conductor looks bemused.
- **13-gold-scale**. The Daoist in luminous gold-trimmed jade robes sits very awkwardly on one pan of an enormous antique merchant balance. A startled goldsmith sets a golden sycee ingot on the other pan.
- **14-name-card**. The Daoist awkwardly holds a comically long elegant purple-and-gold blank name tablet that stretches across a dignified celestial hall and knocks his own hat askew. No calligraphy.
- **15-warranty**. At a glowing celestial court the Daoist wears a modest golden crown and holds up a snapped lightning rod in disbelief as an official offers him a tiny completely blank gilded warranty token. Cosmic gold on ink blue.
